What all of these days have in common

Different people, same three wishes: company that doesn't demand attention, a reason to leave the phone alone, and a little warmth on a routine that can feel like a chore. That's the whole brief Nimo was designed around.

  • Company without effort. He's good company even when nobody is talking.
  • Glanceable moments. Eyes and voice instead of text and taps.
  • A personality that grows. The more time you spend together, the more he becomes yours.

Who Nimo isn't for (yet)

We'd rather you know now than be disappointed later:

  • If you want turn-by-turn navigation or a smart speaker, keep them. Nimo works alongside your assistant, not instead of it. See Nimo vs voice assistants.
  • If you want a robot that roams the house, look at floor robots like Loona. See Nimo vs Loona.

Questions

Is Nimo right for you?

Is Nimo good for long road trips?
That's one of the drives he's designed for: he keeps the cabin lively, plays along with your music, and nudges you to take a break when you've been driving a long time.
Is Nimo good for working from home?
Yes. On a desk he's designed to keep quiet company while you work, nudge you to take breaks, and play a quick game when you need two minutes off.
Can passengers play with Nimo?
Yes. Passengers can pet him and play with him any time. The driver should save touch and games for when the car is parked.
