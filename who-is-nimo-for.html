Who is Nimo for? Ten kinds of people.

Nimo is for anyone who spends long hours in one seat, at a desk or behind the wheel, and would rather not spend them alone. Here are the people we had in mind, what their day looks like, and what Nimo brings to it.

On the road

45 minutes, twice a day

Daily commuters

Same road, same traffic, every day. Nimo turns the commute into a small ritual: a hello in the morning, a sigh in traffic, a celebration when you get there. Messages read aloud mean the phone stays down.

Hours on the highway

Road-trippers

Long drives blur together. Nimo keeps the cabin lively, bobs along to the playlist, and gives you a big, slow yawn when it's been too long since your last stop.

The first year behind the wheel

New drivers

A calm, friendly presence that never shouts directions and never asks you to look at a screen. Glanceable and hands-free, he fits the habits worth building from day one: phone away, eyes up.

School runs and weekend trips

Parents and families

Kids in the back love watching his faces change, and passengers can play with him any time. He's a mood lifter on grumpy mornings and a reason to look forward to the drive.

Strangers in the back seat

Rideshare and taxi drivers

Passengers notice Nimo the moment they get in. He's an icebreaker and a talking point, and he keeps you company between rides when the car is empty.

Empty roads after dark

Night owls and shift workers

Late drives are the loneliest. Nimo's eyes glow softer at night so they don't glare, and he's designed to nudge you toward a break when the drive gets long.

What all of these days have in common

Different people, same three wishes: company that doesn't demand attention, a reason to leave the phone alone, and a little warmth on a routine that can feel like a chore. That's the whole brief Nimo was designed around.

  • Company without effort. He's good company even when nobody is talking.
  • Glanceable moments. Eyes and voice instead of text and taps.
  • A personality that grows. The more time you spend together, the more he becomes yours.

Who Nimo isn't for (yet)

We'd rather you know now than be disappointed later:

  • If you want turn-by-turn navigation or a smart speaker, keep them. Nimo works alongside your assistant, not instead of it. See Nimo vs voice assistants.
