Loona, from KEYi Tech, is a petbot that rolls around your home, follows you from room to room and plays games with the family. Nimo is a different kind of pet: one who stays right where you are, on your desk or in your car.

Where Loona shines

Loona behaves the most like a real pet of any robot here. She comes to find you, follows you around, and turns the living room floor into a playground for games with the kids. If you want a robot that's part of family life at home, Loona is built for exactly that.

Where Nimo is different

Nimo is for the hours you spend sitting still, which for most adults is a lot of them: at a desk, and behind the wheel. He doesn't need floor space and he doesn't wander off. He stays beside you, greets you, keeps you company, nudges you to take a break, and plays a quick game when you have two minutes.

On the dashboard he's designed to be glanceable and hands-free; on your desk he's quiet company while you work. See Nimo on your desk and a day with Nimo on the road.

Which one should you choose?

Choose Loona if

  • You want a robot pet that roams the house.
  • The whole family will play with her.
  • You have open floor space for her to explore.

Choose Nimo if

  • You spend long hours at a desk or in the car.
  • You want company that stays right beside you.
  • Break reminders, games and music appeal.

Questions

Nimo or Loona?

How is Nimo different from Loona?
Loona is a pet robot that rolls around your home and follows you between rooms. Nimo stays in one spot beside you, on your desk or your car dashboard, and keeps you company there.
Which is better for kids?
Loona is built for floor play with the whole family at home. Nimo suits kids who enjoy watching his faces on a desk or from the back seat, with the driver leaving touch and games for when the car is parked.
Can Loona come in the car?
Loona is designed to roam the floor at home, not to ride on a dashboard. Nimo is designed for both your desk and your car.
