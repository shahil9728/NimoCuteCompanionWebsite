A workday with Nimo

  1. 9:00 am · Sit down

    Morning, coworker

    Quiet company

    While you focus, Nimo keeps to himself. He glances around, hums now and then, blinks. He's there in the corner of your eye, the way a cat on the windowsill is there, and he never pings you.

    Mood: calm

    Mood: sleepy

    Desk dance party

    Mood: excited

    See you tomorrow

    You get up to leave, he says goodbye and drifts off to sleep. Tomorrow he'll be just as happy to see you.

Why put a companion on your desk?

Working from home, studying late, long gaming sessions: desks can be lonely places too. Nimo brings the same warmth he brings to the car. A face that's happy to see you, small moments that break up the day, and nothing to scroll.

  • Remote workers get a little company in a quiet home office, and a reminder that lunch is a real thing.
  • Students get a study buddy who cheers on a long session and suggests a break before burnout.
  • Gamers and streamers get a co-star who reacts on the desk, and a game of his own between matches.

More in who Nimo is for.

What he's designed to do on a desk

  • Greet you. A hello when you sit down, a goodbye when you leave.
  • Show the weather at a glance. His eyes tell you about rain or sun before you open an app.
  • Keep quiet company. A small life of his own while you work, with no notifications.
  • Nudge you to move. A yawn and a stretch when you've been sitting for a long time.
  • Play. Tap his head for a quick game on your break.
  • Dance. He moves to your music.
  • Grow with you. His personality changes with how you treat him.

Desk or car? Both.

Nimo is one companion for two places. Keep him beside your keyboard during the week and bring him along for the weekend drive. He's the same Nimo in both: same face, same moods, same personality.

On the road he switches to being glanceable and hands-free; see a day with Nimo on the road and how he's designed for driving.

Questions

Nimo on your desk

Can Nimo be used as a desktop companion?
Yes. Nimo is designed to be good company on your desk as well as on your car dashboard. Same Nimo, same personality, in both places.
What does Nimo do on a desk?
He greets you when you sit down, keeps quiet company while you work, nudges you to take breaks, plays a quick game when you tap his head, dances to your music, and dozes off when you leave.
Will Nimo distract me while I work?
He's designed not to. While you focus he keeps to himself, with the odd glance or blink. He only asks for attention when you've been sitting for a long time, and then just with a yawn and a stretch.
