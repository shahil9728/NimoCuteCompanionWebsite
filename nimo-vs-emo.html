Where EMO shines

EMO is a performer. He roams your desk, peers over the edge, reacts to the people he sees, and dances when music plays. If what you love is watching a tiny robot explore his own world, EMO does it brilliantly, and he's had years to build a fan base that loves him for it.

Where Nimo is different

Nimo is less of a performer and more of a companion. Instead of exploring, he pays attention to you: a hello when you arrive, quiet company while you work, a yawn when you've been sitting too long, a little celebration when you get home.

Because he doesn't walk around, Nimo can do something EMO can't: come along in the car. On the dashboard he stays glanceable and hands-free, and on your desk he's the same Nimo with the same personality. See Nimo on your desk and a day with Nimo on the road.

Which one should you choose?

Choose EMO if

  • You want a robot that walks and explores.
  • He'll only ever live on your desk.
  • You enjoy watching him perform.

Choose Nimo if

  • You want company on your desk and in your car.
  • You'd rather he paid attention to you than to his surroundings.
  • You want gentle break reminders, games and a personality that grows.

Questions

Nimo or EMO?

How is Nimo different from EMO?
EMO is a desk pet that walks around and explores. Nimo stays in one spot and puts all his motion into his face, which lets him work on your desk and on your car dashboard.
Can I use EMO in my car?
EMO wasn't designed for it. He walks around and is made for a flat, still desk, so a moving car isn't his habitat. Nimo is designed to be glanceable and hands-free on a dashboard.
Is Nimo an EMO alternative?
If you like EMO's expressive personality but also want a companion for the car, yes. If you only want a desk pet that wanders and explores, EMO is built exactly for that.
