Where Eilik shines

Eilik is wonderfully simple. Pet it and it melts, and it gets even livelier when several Eiliks are together. It's pure, tiny charm for a desk or a shelf, and it asks very little of you in return.

Where Nimo is different

Nimo keeps the charm and adds a few things. He talks back in short, gentle moments. He connects over WiFi or your phone, so his eyes can tell you about rain before you look outside. He nudges you to take a break when you've been sitting too long, and he has a game picker for when you need two minutes off.

And he isn't stuck on the desk. Nimo is designed to ride on your car dashboard too, glanceable and hands-free, with the same personality you know from your desk. See Nimo on your desk and a day with Nimo on the road.

Which one should you choose?

Choose Eilik if

  • You want the simplest possible cute desk buddy.
  • Touch and reactions are all you need.
  • You'd like a little family of them.

Choose Nimo if

  • You want a companion that talks back.
  • Weather, messages and break reminders appeal.
  • You spend time in the car as well as at a desk.

Questions

Nimo or Eilik?

How is Nimo different from Eilik?
Eilik is a small companion built around touch and cute reactions. Nimo also loves being petted, and adds a voice, things like weather from the outside world, and a design that works on your car dashboard as well as your desk.
Are Nimo and Eilik both desk robots?
Both are happy on a desk. Eilik is designed for the desk or a shelf; Nimo is designed for your desk and your car.
Which is better as a gift?
Eilik is a lovely desk gift for someone who likes small, touchable gadgets. Nimo suits someone who spends time both at a desk and in the car, and wants a companion that comes along.
