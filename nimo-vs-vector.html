Where Vector shines

Vector is a little explorer. He trundles around the table on his treads, looks around, plays with his cube, and answers when you ask him something. Watching him go about his business is half the fun, and plenty of people still love him years after he first appeared.

Where Nimo is different

Nimo trades exploring for attention. He doesn't drive anywhere; he sits beside you and reacts to you: a hello when you arrive, quiet company while you work or drive, a yawn when you need a break, a little celebration when you get home.

That choice is what lets him go where Vector can't. A robot on treads needs a flat, still table. Nimo stays put, so he's designed to ride on your car dashboard as well as live on your desk. See Nimo on your desk and how he's designed for the road.

Which one should you choose?

Choose Vector if

Choose Nimo if

Questions

Nimo or Vector?

How is Nimo different from Vector?
Vector drives around a tabletop on treads, explores and answers questions. Nimo stays beside you in one spot, on your desk or your car dashboard, and focuses on company rather than exploring.
Is Nimo a Vector alternative?
If you loved how alive Vector felt and want that feeling on your desk and in your car, Nimo is designed for you. If you want a robot that roams and plays with his cube, Vector does that.
Can Vector ride in a car?
Vector wasn't designed for it. He drives around on treads and is made for a flat tabletop. Nimo is designed to sit in one secure spot on a dashboard and stay glanceable and hands-free.
