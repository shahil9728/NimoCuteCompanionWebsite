Nimo vs EMO

EMO, from Living AI, is the best-known desk pet robot: a little character that walks around your desk, dances to music and answers to his name. Nimo shares the big idea, an expressive face with a big personality, but stays in one spot, which is what lets him come along in the car too.

Read the full Nimo vs EMO comparison

Nimo vs Eilik

Eilik, from Energize Lab, is a small, touch-first companion that reacts when you pet it and gets livelier with other Eiliks around. Nimo loves being petted too, and adds a voice, a link to the outside world for things like weather, and a design that works on the dashboard.

Read the full Nimo vs Eilik comparison

Nimo vs Vector

Vector, launched by Anki in 2018 and later carried on by Digital Dream Labs, drives around a tabletop on treads and answers questions by voice. Nimo carries the same feeling of a robot that's genuinely alive, without the treads, so he's at home on a desk or a dashboard.

Read the full Nimo vs Vector comparison

Nimo vs Loona

Loona, from KEYi Tech, is a pet-like robot that rolls around your home, follows you between rooms and plays games with the family. Nimo is the opposite kind of pet: he stays where you are, on your desk or in your car, and keeps you company there.

Read the full Nimo vs Loona comparison

The bottom line

All five robots share one belief: technology can feel warm. The question is where you want that warmth. Around the house: Loona. On your desk: EMO, Eilik, Vector or Nimo. On your desk and on the drive: Nimo.

Questions

Choosing a companion robot

What is the best AI companion robot?
It depends on where you'll spend time with it. EMO, Eilik and Vector are popular on desks, Loona roams the floor at home, and Nimo is designed for both your desk and your car dashboard.
Which companion robot works in a car?
Of the five, only Nimo is designed for the car. He stays in one place and keeps every moment glanceable and hands-free while you drive. The others are made for desks and floors at home.
Can Nimo be a desk robot like EMO or Eilik?
Yes. Nimo is designed to work as a desktop companion as well as a car companion, with the same face and personality in both places.
Is Nimo available to buy?
Not yet. Nimo is in development and will ship in limited batches. The waitlist is free and gets you first access when pre-orders open.
